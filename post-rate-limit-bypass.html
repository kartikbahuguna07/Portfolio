Bypassing a Weak Rate Limit on a Password Reset Flow

Jul 20, 2026

A client had a password reset flow that emailed a 6-digit code to the user. On its own that's normal — the problem was how the endpoint handled repeated attempts.

The first few requests came back with the expected 429 Too Many Requests. But watching the response headers closely, the limit reset the moment a specific session-scoped header changed value, rather than being tied to the account or the source IP. Rotating that one header was enough to get a fresh allowance every time.

Why it mattered

A 6-digit numeric code has 1,000,000 possible values. With no durable limit on attempts, an automated client could work through a meaningful fraction of that space well within the code's expiry window — turning a "wait for the email" flow into something closer to a coin flip.

The fix

The team moved rate limiting off anything client-supplied and onto the account identifier itself, with a short lockout after a handful of failed attempts and a lower cap on total codes issued per hour. Simple change, meaningfully different guarantee.
